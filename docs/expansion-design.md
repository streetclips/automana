# Palmwalker — Expansion design: 5 half-grimoires, 4 Nemeses

Status: design draft v0.2 (not yet in the rulebooks or the video). Numbers are playtest starting points.

## 1. Overview

| Colour | Half-grimoire (EN / ES) | Land back | Identity | Signature tools |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| White | Dawnward Order / Orden del Alba | Plains / Llanura {W} | Durable defence, life as recovery | Vigilance, Lifelink, Pacify (tuck an Aura behind an enemy) |
| Blue | Arcane Tide / Marea Arcana | Island / Isla {U} | Control the future | Scry, bend the Omen, Freeze, bounce to the Omen slot |
| Black | Grim Pact / Pacto Sombrío | Swamp / Pantano {B} | Pay life for power | Deathtouch, extra draws, Reanimate from your graveyard |
| Red | Emberkin / Clan Ascua | Mountain / Montaña {R} | Speed and burn | Haste, direct damage |
| Green | Wildwood / Monteverde | Forest / Bosque {G} | Big bodies | Trample, pump, mana creature |

| Nemesis (EN / ES) | Colour | Archetype | Difficulty | Phase-change effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Vorthrax, the Ash Tyrant / Vorthrax, el Tirano de Ceniza | Red | Aggro: haste and burn | ★★ | Rekindle: 3 Ruin cards back face-down |
| Vaskara, the Brood Mother / Vaskara, la Madre Enjambre | Green | Swarm: many bodies per turn | ★★ | Hatching: execute the Omen at once |
| Morwen, the Lich Queen / Morwen, la Reina Liche | Black | Attrition: dead things return | ★★★ | Uprising: 2 Ruin creatures return to the Table |
| The Mirror Archmage / El Archimago Espejo | Blue | Control: taxes and counters | ★★★ | Reflection: bounce your best creature, 2 Ruin cards back |

Replay space: 10 colour pairs × 4 Nemeses × Normal/Heroic = 80 configurations.

Product: 60 Grimoire cards + 1 Kael + 4 Nemesis cards + 60 Threat cards + 1 reference = 126 cards. A single game still uses only 41 (24 + 15 + Kael + Nemesis).

## 2. Rules added by the expansion

All new rules stay table-free: no counters, no tokens.

### 2.1 Keywords

| Keyword (EN / ES) | Rule | In-hand handling |
|---|---|---|
| Vigilance / Vigilancia | Attacking doesn't lower this creature. | It simply stays raised. |
| Lifelink / Vínculo vital | Damage dealt by this creature also makes its controller gain that much life. | You: graveyard → library. Nemesis: Ruin → face-down bottom. |
| Deathtouch / Toque mortal | Any damage this creature deals to a creature is lethal. | — |

### 2.2 Actions and states

| Action (EN / ES) | Rule | In-hand handling |
|---|---|---|
| Scry N / Adivinar N | Look at the top N library cards; put any of them at the bottom in any order. | Peek at their spell side, then slide them between library and graveyard. |
| Pacify / Pacificar | An Aura you tuck behind a Nemesis creature: it can't attack or block. | The Aura sticks up behind the enemy card; it goes to your graveyard if the creature leaves. |
| Freeze / Congelar | Lower a Nemesis creature and turn it upside down. At its next untap it turns upright instead of rising. | Upside-down = frozen for one extra turn. |
| Counter reminder / Recordatorio de contraconjuro | A Nemesis "Counterspell" stays in the Table fan, turned sideways, until it counters your next spell. Then it goes face-down to the bottom of the Threat deck. | Sideways card = pending effect. |
| Nemesis lifelink | When a Nemesis creature with Lifelink deals damage, return that many cards from the Ruin face-down to the bottom of the Threat deck. | Symmetric with yours. |

Kael stays as the universal Walker for every colour pair. Five colour-specific Walkers are a possible later expansion.

## 4. New Nemeses (15 Threat cards each, Phase 2 at 7 or fewer face-down)

All Nemeses use the same skeleton as Vorthrax: 14 life after the first Omen, one Omen per turn, the same attack/block/target rules, and Heroic = Nemesis creatures get +1/+0.

### 4.1 Vaskara, the Brood Mother / Vaskara, la Madre Enjambre (green, swarm)

- **Phase 1 — BROOD / NIDADA:** at the end of each Nemesis turn, if Vaskara has 2 or fewer creatures, she also executes the new Omen and reveals another.
- **Flip — HATCHING / ECLOSIÓN:** execute the Omen immediately and reveal another.
- **Phase 2 — FRENZY / FRENESÍ:** Nemesis creatures get +1/+0 and Trample. Brood stays active.
- **Tension:** she floods the Table fast (Overflow damage matters), but every extra reveal costs her life. Sweepers and big blockers shine.

### 4.2 Morwen, the Lich Queen / Morwen, la Reina Liche (black, attrition)

- **Phase 1 — GRAVE TITHE / DIEZMO DE TUMBA:** at the end of each Nemesis turn, if the Ruin holds 3 or more creatures, return the highest-power one to the Table.

## 5. Matchups and colour pairs

| Nemesis | What beats it | Recommended pairs |
|---|---|---|
| Vorthrax | Lifelink and sturdy blockers survive the burn race | W/G, W/R |
| Vaskara | Sweepers (Volcanic Roar, Shield Formation for trades) and big trample | R/G, R/W |
| Morwen | Lock instead of kill (Pacify, Freeze, bounce) plus direct damage | W/U, U/R |
| Mirror Archmage | Cheap threats, many 1-drops, few big spells | W/B, R/B |

Five showcase pairs:

1. **R/G — Brute force** (base box): haste, pump, trample.
2. **W/B — Life as a resource**: lifelink refills what black's pacts spend.
3. **U/R — Arcane tempo**: flyers, burn, and Bend Fate to dodge the worst Omens.
4. **B/G — Cycle of life**: damage fills your graveyard, Reanimate brings back Crashhorn.
5. **W/U — Control fate**: Pacify, Freeze, and flyers that never need to trade.

None right now.

Resolved:

1. Kael stays as the only Walker for now. Colour-specific Walkers remain a possible later expansion.
2. Banish into Light keeps the +1 life drawback and costs {1}{W}. At {2}{W} it was strictly worse than Assassinate; now it is cheap removal with a price, and the key anti-Morwen tool because the creature never reaches the Ruin.
